<h2 align="center"><strong>Compilation of Real-World LLM & AI Agent Use Cases in Financial Services</strong></h2>


### Context  
Large language models (LLMs) and AI agents are transforming financial services: from streamlining regulatory compliance and accelerating deal analysis to personalizing customer experiences and automating complex workflows across banking, trading, and asset management.

This repository curates real-world LLM and agentic AI implementations in finance, showcasing enterprise platforms, specialized models, and integrated solutions deployed by leading financial institutions worldwide.

    - Addresses life, health, property, and casualty insurance, along with reinsurance, underwriting automation, and actuarial modeling. Features AI-driven claims processing, fraud detection, personalized policy recommendations, and broader risk assessment tools.

Each example can be classified into one of 3 categories based on type of use case:
- **Enterprise Platform**: Large-scale LLM deployments spanning multiple lines of business or the entire organization. These implementations typically address a broad range of tasks—such as customer support, internal operations, and compliance—within a unified, centrally managed framework.
- **Fine-Tuned Model**: Models either pre-trained or fine-tuned on focused datasets (e.g., regulatory filings, financial news) to achieve deep domain expertise. By narrowing the training data to specific tasks and terminology, they deliver higher accuracy and more relevant insights than general-purpose models.
- **Integrated Solution**: Vendor-provided or off-the-shelf AI solutions deployed for specific workflows and use cases. Financial institutions leverage ready-made integrations, cloud services, and prompt engineering to streamline tasks—such as AI-powered chatbots or document processing—without extensive internal development.
